RemoteTech Wormhole Bridge 0.6.0

RemoteTech Wormhole Bridge lets RemoteTech route signals through paired KEX
wormholes. Relay placement bands adapt to each wormhole sphere of influence.
Red guide rings show the valid radial limits, while magenta cone edges show the
compatible directional coverage only between those limits.

The plugin discovers KEX wormholes and loaded or unloaded RemoteTech endpoints,
validates bidirectional dish coverage, and inserts eligible logical edges into
the RemoteTech graph. It works in Flight and the Tracking Station.

Required:
- Kerbal Space Program 1.12.5
- RemoteTech 1.9.12
- RemoteTech Overhaul 0.1 or later
- Kopernicus Expansion Continued (KEX-Wormholes)
- HarmonyKSP
- ModuleManager

RemoteTech Overhaul:
https://github.com/Wid4er/KSP-RemoteTechOverhaul/releases

WormholeSignalBridge is not required.
