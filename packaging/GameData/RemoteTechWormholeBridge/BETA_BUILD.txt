RemoteTech Wormhole Bridge 0.5.0 beta 1

Operational relay band: calculated independently for each wormhole from its
transition surface and sphere of influence, with a 300 km desired maximum.
Map visuals: links/cones use magenta #FF4FD8; guide rings use red #FF3030. Cone
edges are truncated between the local inner and outer limits, matching the valid
relay band shown by the rings around the selected wormhole.

This beta mutates RemoteTech's logical graph when a validated bidirectional
wormhole pair is active. It does not emit RemoteTech renderer events and does
not draw an interstellar wormhole line. Its own renderer draws the two local
segments of active bridges and the projected cones of every accepted endpoint
in the selected wormhole pair, whether or not that endpoint is currently paired.
Discovery, graph injection and rendering run in Flight and Tracking Station.

It discovers KEX wormholes and loaded or unloaded RemoteTech endpoints, records
the exact orbital state around a KEX jump, and validates bidirectional dish
coverage through paired mouths. Active pairs are inserted into RemoteTech with
an effective local path cost.

Required:
- Kerbal Space Program 1.12.5
- RemoteTech
- RemoteTech Overhaul 0.1 or later
- Kopernicus Expansion Continued (KEX-Wormholes)
- HarmonyKSP
- ModuleManager

WormholeSignalBridge is not required.
